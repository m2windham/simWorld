## Storyteller probe - the threat curve over 60 in-game days at founding scale, populated world (`--solo false`)


> One arm: **watched and played**. The founding settlement is opened, so its citizens run at Full tier and a threat arrives on a real map. **World: populated** - rival civilizations are generated at time zero, as a player's world has them. Table 0 lists who they are and where they stand with the player. Founding band 20 (spec 5b.3 allows 20-40), seed 31337, storyteller and difficulty as `Game.NewGame` defaults them. **This suite changes no tuning constant and is not a target** - see its class doc, and `CLAUDE.md` on what happens to a game tuned against its own instruments.
